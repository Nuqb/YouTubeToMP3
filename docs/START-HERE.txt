YOUTUBE TO MP3 - WINDOWS

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted YouTubeToMP3 folder.
3. Double-click YouTubeToMP3.exe. The converter opens in your browser.

Paste a video or track link, click Convert, then Download .mp3.
Save your MP3 promptly: temporary converted files expire after 30 seconds.
Your browser saves the MP3 in its usual download location.

Keep the launcher window open while converting. Close it to stop the app.
The Open converter button reopens the browser if you close the tab.

No Python, Git, FFmpeg installation, or administrator access is required.
Keep the entire extracted folder together, including _internal.
Requires Windows 10/11, 64-bit, a browser, and an internet connection.

This release is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.
Only run a download you trust from https://github.com/Nuqb/YouTubeToMP3/releases
Do not disable antivirus protection.

If something fails, download the newest release and try a public link.
Private, subscription-only, or restricted content may be unavailable.
Spotify and Beatstars links search YouTube for a matching track.
Startup logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\YouTubeToMP3\app.log
Help: https://github.com/Nuqb/YouTubeToMP3/issues

Only download content you own or have permission to download.
